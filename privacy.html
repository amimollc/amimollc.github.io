Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 24, 2025

Amimo LLC (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use the Amimo Discovery app (“the App”).

1. Information We Collect

  • IP Address & Location: We collect your IP address and approximate location (country level) to serve relevant local news.
  • Device Information: Basic device type, browser, and operating system to ensure compatibility.
  • User‑provided data: If you save articles, that data is stored locally on your device (not on our servers).

2. How We Use Information

  • To deliver personalised news content.
  • To improve the App’s performance and user experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Third‑Party Services

We use the following third‑party services that may process your data:

  • RSS2JSON API – fetches RSS feeds. Their privacy policy: https://rss2json.com/privacy
  • Google AdSense – displays advertisements. They may use cookies or device identifiers to serve relevant ads. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • IP location APIs (ipapi.co / ipinfo.io) – used to detect your country for local news.

4. Cookies

We do not set persistent cookies ourselves. However, third‑party services (like AdSense) may set cookies for analytics and ad targeting. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser.

5. Your Choices

  • You can disable location detection at any time (your browser settings).
  • You can clear saved articles and cached data within the App.
  • You may opt out of personalized ads via Google’s Ad Settings: https://adssettings.google.com

6. Data Security

We use HTTPS to encrypt data in transit. Your saved articles are stored only on your device – we do not have access to them.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL.
