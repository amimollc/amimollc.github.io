About Amimo LLC

Welcome to Amimo Discovery – your intelligent news aggregator and personal information hub.

Who We Are

Amimo LLC is a small technology company dedicated to creating simple, useful, and privacy‑respecting tools. Our mission is to connect you with the stories that matter, from your local community to the world.

What We Do

  • News Aggregation – We bring together RSS feeds from trusted sources worldwide, covering Local, World, Politics, Technology, Sports, Entertainment, Business, and Health.
  • Offline Support – Save articles to read later, even without an internet connection.
  • File Management – Built‑in tools to help you manage your device storage (browse, scan junk, clean cache).
  • Live News – Watch live news streams from YouTube.
  • Privacy First – We do not store your personal data. Your saved articles stay on your device.

Our Values

  • Transparency – We believe in being open about how the App works.
  • User Control – You are in charge of your data and your experience.
  • Simplicity – Clean, fast, and easy to use.

Technology Stack

  • Pure HTML/CSS/JavaScript – no external frameworks.
  • RSS2JSON proxy for feed fetching.
  • File System Access API for file management.
  • Service Worker for offline capabilities and background sync.
